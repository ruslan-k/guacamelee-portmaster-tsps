-------------------

backbuffer transition; program18 is already black before its selected draw.

Program86 input state
---------------------

  program86 sampler0 guest texture=2, native texture=2
  realization reports current native binding/actual texture 83 on first cycle
  texture2 was created as 1280x720 RGBA storage and attached as FBO2 color0

Program86 is fullscreen geometry and blend is disabled at the causal draw.
The result is therefore consistent with program86 sampling a black main-image
texture and writing that result over the non-black FBO0 point. No presenter or
swap behavior is involved.

Control chronology
------------------
Program2 with white color can produce (109,167,160,255), but its output is later
overwritten by program86. Program18's zero RGB vertex color explains why its
own selected primitive does not make the point non-black, but it is not the first
transition responsible for the visible black frame.
