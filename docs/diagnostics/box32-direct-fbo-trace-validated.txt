Interpretation of the first FBO setup:

- color renderbuffer: `GL_RGBA8` (`0x8058`), 1280x720;
- depth/stencil renderbuffer: `0x88f0` (`GL_DEPTH24_STENCIL8`), 1280x720;
- the same renderbuffer is attached to `GL_DEPTH_STENCIL_ATTACHMENT`, `GL_DEPTH_ATTACHMENT`, and `GL_STENCIL_ATTACHMENT`;
- status after color-only attachment: `0x8cd7` (`GL_FRAMEBUFFER_INCOMPLETE_MISSING_ATTACHMENT`);
- status after depth/stencil attachments: `0x8cdd` (`GL_FRAMEBUFFER_UNSUPPORTED`).

## Current blocker

The validated FBO+texture trace proves the first game FBO sequence and the texture allocation inputs. The first offscreen FBO attaches texture 1 at level 0 after the game has issued level-0 texture uploads; later FBOs attach texture IDs 89, 90, and 91. The trace still reports `0x8cd7` for those FBO checks, while the first depth/stencil setup reports `0x8cdd` after the `0x88f0` depth-stencil renderbuffer is attached to three attachment points.

This narrows the failure to a later render pass/menu transition rather than generic SDL/KMS startup. The user reports that the image reaches the menu and then turns black. A separate early/late capture run in this session produced two identical full-black PNGs, so it does not independently prove the menu frame; the user's timing report is retained as physical evidence and is not replaced by that capture.

The next bounded fix candidate is to correlate the exact texture record (level-0 dimensions/format/type) with FBO attachment and then test one render-path change at a time. Do not change production behavior or declare visible gameplay until an actual menu and gameplay frame are captured.
